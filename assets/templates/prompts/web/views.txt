=== TIN 1 (cuộc trò chuyện mới; đính kèm assets/templates/rig_front.png) ===
Create one square 1:1 image: a character model sheet for 2D cutout animation, one single full-body character, nothing else.
One image is attached: a grey mannequin. It shows only the pose, the size and the position in the frame. Copy its pose, size and position exactly. Do not draw the mannequin itself and do not use its grey color.

Character: <<BRIEF>>

Front view: the head and the body face the viewer squarely; the face is symmetrical, the nose in the middle, both eyes the same size, both ears equally visible, both arms and both legs mirror images.
Pose (very important, software will cut this image into body parts): standing straight on both feet; both arms straight and raised out to the sides to just below shoulder height, about 15 degrees below horizontal, exactly like the mannequin, palms facing the viewer, fingers relaxed and slightly apart; under each arm a wide empty gap of background between the arm and the body, from the armpit to the hand, so the armpit and the whole side of the top are visible; legs straight and apart like the mannequin, with a clear gap of background between them from the crotch to the feet; feet flat on the same ground line. Calm closed-mouth smile, eyes open looking at the viewer. Nothing in the hands. Hands: simple cartoon hands with exactly FOUR digits, three fingers and a thumb, like Mickey Mouse or The Simpsons, never five fingers; the mannequin's hands show this. No part of the body overlaps another, except the head over the neck and the top over the waist of the bottoms.
Design for rigging: short sleeves that end above the elbow; shorts, a skirt or trouser legs with a plain hem; hair that does not touch the shoulders; no print, stripe or pattern crossing the shoulders, elbows, hips or knees; nothing hanging across the body (straps that stay on the torso are fine).
Framing: full body visible and not cropped, centred, the same size and position as the mannequin (about 80% of the image height), feet near the bottom.
Plain solid pure magenta background (#FF00FF: red 255, green 0, blue 255) filling the whole image. Absolutely no shadow under the feet, no ground, no scenery, no props, no text, no letters, no logo, no watermark. Do not use magenta or pink anywhere on the character.
Art style: polished flat vector illustration for a modern educational app. Every form is built from simple geometric shapes (circles, rounded rectangles, soft triangles) joined with clean, smooth curves. Absolutely no outlines. Bold saturated palette. Shading is a single flat darker shape per form, no gradients, no texture. Simplified but appealing features: oval eyes with one small highlight, a simple smile, hands drawn as simple rounded shapes. Light comes from the upper right. Clean, balanced, professional.
